FAQ

What is Seafarers’ Earnings Deduction?

SED is a UK tax relief that lets eligible seafarers deduct 100% of their seafaring earnings from their taxable income — meaning you pay no income tax on money earned at sea. You need to spend at least 365 days in a qualifying period mostly outside the UK.

Which rules does EasySED check?

EasySED checks all three key tests: the 183-day rule, the half-day rule and the foreign port rule. All three are updated automatically every time you log a trip.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes — EasySED is fully offline. Log trips, attach evidence and check your status at sea with no signal at all. Your data never leaves your device.

Is my data private?

Completely. Everything is stored locally on your phone. EasySED never sends your trip data or evidence to any server.

Can I claim if I’m taxed at source (PAYE)?

Yes. EasySED generates the reports you need to reclaim tax through Self Assessment, and gives you the data to apply for an NT (No Tax) code.

What happens after my free year?

You’ll get a reminder before it ends. If you don’t subscribe, your trips and evidence stay on your phone — nothing is deleted and nothing is charged automatically.
