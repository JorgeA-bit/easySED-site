FAQ

Common questions,
straight answers.

What is Seafarers' Earnings Deduction?
SED is a UK tax relief that lets eligible seafarers deduct 100% of their seafaring earnings from their taxable income — meaning you pay no income tax on money earned at sea. You need to spend at least 365 days in a qualifying period mostly outside the UK. Read our full guide →
Which SED rules does easySED check?
easySED checks all three key tests: the 182-day rule (you can't spend more than 182 days in the UK in your qualifying period), the half-day rule (UK days must be fewer than non-UK days) and the foreign port rule (your voyages must begin or end outside the UK). All three are checked automatically as you log each trip.
Does it work without an internet connection?
Yes — easySED is fully offline. You can log trips, attach evidence and review your status at sea with no signal at all. Your data is stored locally on your device and never sent to a remote server.
Is my data private?
Completely. easySED stores everything locally on your phone — it doesn't send your trip data or evidence to any server. You have full control over what you share and when.
Can I claim if I'm taxed at source (PAYE)?
Yes. easySED tracks the tax you've paid and generates the reports you need to reclaim it through Self Assessment. You can also use the data to apply for an NT (No Tax) code so your employer stops deducting tax at source.
What happens after my free year?
You'll get a reminder before your free year ends. If you don't subscribe, your trips and evidence stay on your phone — you can still view them and make a backup. Nothing is deleted, and nothing is charged automatically.
