A simple way to track eligibility and evidence for SED.

easySED is built for UK seafarers who need confidence in their day counts and paperwork. It's designed to keep your records consistent and exportable.

Designed around real rules

Tracks UK vs non-UK days using the "outside the UK at midnight" approach, and supports the typical qualifying-period workflow.

Built for documentation

Evidence is not an afterthought. The goal is to keep your supporting info organised from day one.

Accurate calculations

Automatically calculates your qualifying days using HMRC's UK-midnight rule, eliminating manual counting errors.

Export-ready reports

Generate HMRC-ready documentation and evidence packs when you reach your 365-day qualifying period.

Who is easySED for?

easySED is designed for UK seafarers who need to track their days outside the UK for Seafarers' Earnings Deduction (SED) tax relief. Whether you're working on cruise ships, cargo vessels, or offshore platforms, easySED helps you maintain accurate records.

Important: easySED is not tax advice. It's a record-keeping and document-generation tool. Always consult with a qualified tax advisor for specific tax guidance.
