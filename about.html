Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, straight answers.

Can I claim if I'm being taxed at source (PAYE)?

Yes. easySED tracks the tax you've paid and generates the reports you need to reclaim every penny from HMRC.

Does it handle provisional claims?

Absolutely. If you're mid-way through your first 365 days when the tax year ends, the app provides the exact data needed to file a provisional claim safely.

Is my data safe?

Privacy by Design. Your data is stored locally on your own device, not on a remote server. You have total control over your information — it stays on your phone, exactly where it belongs.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. easySED is fully offline-capable. You can log trips, attach evidence and review your status at sea with no signal required.

What is the Half-Day Rule?

The Half-Day Rule determines whether a day counts as a UK day or a non-UK day based on where you are at midnight. easySED applies this automatically — you just log your movements. See our guides for a full explanation.

Who is easySED for?

Built for UK seafarers.

easySED is designed for UK seafarers who need to track their days outside the UK for Seafarers' Earnings Deduction (SED) tax relief. Whether you're working on cruise ships, cargo vessels, or offshore platforms, easySED helps you maintain accurate records and build a complete audit trail — without the spreadsheet headache.

Important: easySED is a record-keeping and document-generation tool, not tax advice. Always consult with a qualified tax advisor for guidance specific to your situation.
